About Us

Who we are, what we do and why flood awareness matters in Taungoo.

Who We Are

We are Current 4, a group of Year 9 students working on a Project-Based Learning (PBL) task about Disaster Risk Reduction. We chose flooding because many families in Taungoo live close to the river and can be affected during the rainy season.

Our goal is simple: help households understand flood risks, prepare early and recover safely.

Our Mission

What we want this website to achieve.

Raise Awareness

Explain why floods happen and why Taungoo riverside communities are at risk.

Share Preparedness Guides

Give clear, easy steps that any family can follow before, during and after a flood.

Support Communities

Encourage neighbours to share information, help each other and reduce damage together.

Why Taungoo?

Taungoo is a town in the Bago Region of Myanmar near the Sittaung River. During heavy monsoon rain, river levels can rise quickly and affect homes, farmland and roads.

Disaster Risk Reduction means reducing the damage before a disaster happens. By learning and planning, communities can stay safer and recover faster.
