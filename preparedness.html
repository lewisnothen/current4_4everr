Flood Preparedness Guide

Simple steps for riverside households to stay safe before, during and after a flood.

Be Ready in 3 Stages

Preparing in advance is the best way to protect your family. Use this guide to plan what to do before, during and after a flood.

Talk through the plan with everyone at home, including children and elderly family members.

Before & During

Before the Flood

  • Listen to weather news and local warnings
  • Make a family plan and meeting place
  • Pack an emergency go-bag
  • Move valuables and documents to a high place
  • Know your nearest safe shelter

During the Flood

  • Move to higher ground early
  • Do not walk or drive through floodwater
  • Switch off electricity if safe to do so
  • Keep a radio or phone for updates
  • Stay together and help others

Emergency Go-Bag Checklist

Keep it packed in a waterproof bag near the door.

  • Drinking water and ready-to-eat food
  • Torch and spare batteries
  • Battery or hand-crank radio
  • First-aid kit and personal medicine
  • Copies of ID and important papers in a waterproof bag
  • Phone, power bank and charging cable
  • Warm clothes and a blanket
  • Whistle and some cash

Make a Family Flood Plan

Four steps every household can do.

Know your risk

Find out how close your home is to the river and how high past floods have reached.

Choose a safe place

Pick a higher place and two routes to reach it. Practice the walk.

Share contacts

Write down family and local contacts and keep a copy in each bag.

Practice together

Review the plan before the rainy season so everyone knows what to do.

Tip: Add your local ward or township emergency contacts here so your family can find them quickly.
