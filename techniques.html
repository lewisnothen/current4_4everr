Flood Techniques

Simple, low-cost ways to protect your home, your health and your community.

Practical Flood Techniques

These low-cost techniques can reduce damage to your home and help your family stay safe. Choose the ones that suit your house and your community.

Protect Your Home

Reduce water damage inside the house.

Raise Valuables

Store documents, electronics and food on high shelves, upper floors or raised platforms.

Sandbags and Barriers

Place sandbags at doors and low openings to slow the water.

Raised Floors and Stilts

Homes built on raised foundations or stilts keep water below living areas.

Waterproof Storage

Seal important papers and clothes in plastic bags or sealed containers.

Water, Food and Health

Stay healthy during and after a flood.

Store Safe Water

Keep clean drinking water in covered containers before the flood.

Boil or Treat Water

Boil water for at least one minute or use purification tablets.

Protect Food

Keep dry food high up. Throw away anything touched by floodwater.

Community Techniques

Work together to reduce flood impact.

Clear Drains Together

Organise clean-ups of drains and ditches before the rains start.

Plant Trees

Trees along riverbanks help hold soil and slow runoff.

Early Warning Network

Share warnings by phone, radio and word of mouth so everyone gets time to move.

Do and Don't

Quick reminders.

Do

  • Move to higher ground early
  • Keep a go-bag ready
  • Follow official warnings
  • Help neighbours

Don't

  • Walk through floodwater
  • Touch electrical items when wet
  • Wait until the last minute
  • Drink untreated water
